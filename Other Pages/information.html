About us

We are a football school open to everyone. We have limited places, but we do not select the best children to form competitive teams. We accept everyone, regardless of their knowledge of football, and we complete the teams with the children who want to sign up. We are a non-profit school, and what we try to do is teach the sport we are passionate about. If you want to be part of the team, sign up now.
