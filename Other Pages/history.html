History of the club

The club started in 2018 and was already successful in its first year, as the cadet category was promoted that same year. The club is still active today and after the success of the first season, several years of promotions followed in different categories such as the benjamin and prebenjamin. The last 2 years we have been fighting to achieve great success again, coming close to entering the top 3 last year with the alevín category. The club continues to grow, we train children from an early age, if you want to be part of the history of the club, sign up now.
